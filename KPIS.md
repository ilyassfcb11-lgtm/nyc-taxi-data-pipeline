# KPIs

Metrics used across the dashboard and mart tables.

| KPI | What it measures |
|-----|-----------------|
| Total Trips | Count of cleaned taxi trips |
| Total Revenue | Sum of total_amount across all trips |
| Avg Revenue per Trip | Average fare including tips and fees |
| Revenue per Mile | How efficiently each mile generates revenue |
| Revenue per Hour | Hourly earning rate per trip |
| Peak Load Factor | Peak hour trips ÷ average hourly trips — measures demand spikiness |
| Fleet Priority Score | 0–100 composite score: 50% volume + 30% revenue + 20% efficiency — tells you where to send more taxis |
| Zone Utilization Proxy | Zone trips ÷ average across all zones — above 1 means above-average demand |
| CBD Congestion Fee | NYC congestion pricing fee collected on trips entering the Manhattan CBD (new Jan 2025) |
