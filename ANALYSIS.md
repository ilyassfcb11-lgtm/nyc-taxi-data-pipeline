# Key Findings

Analysis of 44.2M NYC Yellow Taxi trips from 2025.

---

**Demand peaks at 6PM** with over 3M trips in that hour across the year. The 5-7PM window accounts for roughly 22% of all daily demand — the clearest operational signal in the dataset.

**Manhattan dominates at 73% of all pickups**, but outer-borough trips earn 18% more per mile on average because they tend to be longer, highway-based routes with fewer short hops diluting the average.

**Airport routes are the highest-revenue routes in the network.** JFK and LaGuardia pickups average $58.42 per trip vs $28.84 citywide — more than double. Fleet allocation toward airports during travel peaks is clearly justified by revenue.

**Weekday vs weekend patterns shift completely.** Total trip counts are similar, but weekdays peak at commute hours (8AM, 6PM) while weekends peak at 2-3AM and noon — nightlife and late morning activity.

**CBD congestion pricing** (new in January 2025) generated $24.2M in fees across the year. Trips entering the Manhattan CBD show slightly shorter durations on average, suggesting some route adjustment behavior already emerging in the data.

**Zone efficiency varies widely.** The top 10 zones by fleet priority score account for 31% of all revenue despite covering less than 4% of zones — strong signal for where to concentrate resources.
