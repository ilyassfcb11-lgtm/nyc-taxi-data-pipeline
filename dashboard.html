🏙️ Manhattan dominates by volume, airports win on revenue
87% of trips · $24.77 avg
Manhattan accounts for 38.1M of 44.2M trips but earns only $24.77/trip on average. Queens (4.2M trips) earns $61.68/trip due to JFK ($81.66) and LaGuardia ($69.74). A single airport run is worth 3.3× a Manhattan run.
🌙 Evening rush (6 PM) is peak volume, but 5 AM earns most
6 PM: 2.97M trips · 5 AM: $53.18/trip
Trip volume peaks at 6 PM with 2.97M trips. But 5 AM has the highest average revenue per trip ($53.18) due to airport runs before the morning rush. Drivers who shift to early mornings earn significantly more per trip.
📅 August slump is structural, not random
Aug: 3.18M trips — yearly low
August is the lowest month in 2025 (3.18M trips vs. May peak at 4.09M). This is a structural NYC pattern: affluent residents leave the city for summer. Fleet managers should expect reduced demand every August.
🚦 CBD Congestion Pricing launched Jan 2025 — $24.2M collected
$24.2M in congestion fees
NYC's Central Business District Congestion Pricing is fully live in 2025 data. Fees peak in May ($2.25M) and track monthly trip volume closely. This is a new revenue stream not present in 2024 data — a key differentiator in this dataset.
📍 Upper East Side is highest-volume, but inefficient per mile
1.99M trips · $21.25 avg · 1.8 mi avg
The Upper East Side South zone leads in trips (1.99M) but has short trips averaging 1.8 miles at $21.25/trip. In contrast, JFK has fewer trips (1.88M) but earns $81.66/trip at 4× the distance. Resource allocation should account for this trade-off.
💳 67% of trips are credit card — tip behavior follows
67% credit card · avg tip ~$3
Credit card trips dominate (67%) and are strongly correlated with higher tips. Cash trips have near-zero recorded tips due to TLC data limitations. Airport credit card trips have the highest tip averages, reinforcing their revenue advantage.
Operational Recommendations
  • 🕔
    Shift fleet allocation toward 5–7 AM airport window
    Revenue per trip is 84% higher than the day average. JFK runs at 5 AM average $81+ vs $28 city-wide. Target outer borough drivers already near airports.
  • 📍
    Reduce idle time in Upper East Side during off-peak hours
    Upper East Side South has the highest trip count but peak demand concentrates in the 3–6 PM window. Off-peak, drivers would earn more repositioning to Midtown Center or Times Square.
  • 📆
    Plan reduced fleet capacity in August — structural lull
    August shows a predictable 22% drop from the May peak every year. Operations teams can use this window for vehicle maintenance without impacting service levels.
  • 🚦
    Monitor CBD congestion fee impact on trip demand in lower Manhattan
    $24.2M in CBD fees collected across 2025. The fee's behavioral impact on trip demand (shorter trips, fewer downtown pickups) should be monitored quarter-over-quarter.
